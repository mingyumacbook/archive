The S/S 27 Key Colours have landed

The latest WGSN x Coloro Key Colours forecast is here – here’s what you need to know.
Jul 18, 2025
WGSN x Coloro - Key Colours S/S 27

The five colours that will define the Spring/Summer season in two years’ time have landed. Meet Luminous Blue, Energy Orange, Pop Pink, Meadowland Green and Clay. Want to know how we forecast the colours? Go behind the scenes in our exclusive blog post.

Luminous Blue

 

Chosen as the Colour of the Year for 2027, this confident and wise hue will work hard across all categories as a gender inclusive, transseasonal shade. Whether it’s fashion in the form of occasion- or activewear, or across consumer tech with lacquered and metallic surfaces, prepare for Luminous Blue’s presence to be known.

Energy Orange

 

Courageous, dynamic and optimistic, Energy Orange strikes a balance between positive and practical. Provoking themes of rescue and repair, this shade will be seen across accessories and will be popular in sports and outdoor. For interiors, Energy Orange embraces #NewRetro themes for furniture and lighting.

Pop Pink

 

Popular by name, Pop Pink is playful and empowered. Resonating especially with youth and kidswear, this colour is ideal for gender inclusive ranges across all product categories. With a comforting effect, in the interiors space, expect to see hyper-tactility in soft furnishings.

Meadowland Green

 

A colour categorised as balanced, serene and safe, Meadowland Green has a nourishing quality that connects with nature. For beauty, this is extended to themes of ancient wellness and spirituality in packaging. For fashion, this hue boasts versatility, working across menswear, womenswear, footwear and accessories.

Clay

 

Resilient and trusting, Clay is a dependable crowd pleaser that can be used to design for longer lifespans, bringing a sense of luxe futurism. For interiors, Clay will be drenched from floor to ceiling, leaning into its transformative and modern nature. Across consumer tech and automotive, expect a multisensory environment of metallic accents and hyper-tactile finishes.

There you have it. With 93% accuracy across all our forecasts, prepare to see these colours in S/S 27. Want to access more trend insights across colour and beyond? Book a WGSN demo today.
