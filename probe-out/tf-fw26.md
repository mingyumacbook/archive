# Fall 2026 Womenswear Trends from the Runways (FW26)

Source: https://tfashion.ai/runway/fw26
Data refreshed: October 7, 2026

Biggest risers at FW26 womenswear: tailored pants (19.1% → 22.3% of looks), poplin (8.4% → 10.1%) and charcoal (6.5% → 8.0%). Most common: black (54.1%), chocolate (22.5%) and tailored pants (22.3%).

Measured across 13,370 looks from 400 designers.

## In the press

We read 417 articles on FW26 womenswear from Vogue, WWD, Marie Claire UK and 78 more. The trends they named most: leather, black, fur, lace and floral. Leather (10.1%), black (54.1%) and floral (9.5%) held steady.
