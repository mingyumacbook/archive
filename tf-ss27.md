# Spring 2027 Womenswear Trends from the Runways (SS27)

Source: https://tfashion.ai/runway/ss27
Data refreshed: October 7, 2026

Biggest risers at SS27 womenswear: straight skirts (4.9% → 10.3% of looks), white (23.6% → 28.9%) and dropped dress (0.6% → 4.8%). Most common: black (35.4%), Avant-Garde (32.1%) and white (28.9%).

Measured across 11,487 looks from 338 designers.

## In the press

We read 513 articles on SS27 womenswear from Vogue, The Business of Fashion, WWD and 76 more. The trends they named most: sheer, floral, blazer, black and white. The data backs floral (11.2% → 14.4% of looks) and white (23.6% → 28.9%). Black (35.4%) held steady.
