# Spring 2027 Menswear Trends from the Runways (SS27)

Source: https://tfashion.ai/runway/ss27/menswear
Data refreshed: October 7, 2026

Biggest risers at SS27 menswear: tailored pants (35.3% → 42.9% of looks), drop shoulder jacket (4.4% → 11.7%) and high-rise pants (8.6% → 14.7%). Most common: twill (57.5%), tailored pants (42.9%) and black (39.7%).

Measured across 5,169 looks from 177 designers.
