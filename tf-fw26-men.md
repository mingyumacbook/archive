# Fall 2026 Menswear Trends from the Runways (FW26)

Source: https://tfashion.ai/runway/fw26/menswear
Data refreshed: October 7, 2026

Biggest risers at FW26 menswear: wide leg pants (28.4% → 32.2% of looks), charcoal (12.7% → 16.1%) and poplin (23.9% → 27.2%). Most common: black (56.0%), wide leg pants (32.2%) and chocolate (27.4%).

Measured across 6,064 looks from 262 designers.

## In the press

We read 265 articles on FW26 menswear from Vogue, WWD, GQ and 58 more. The trends they named most: leather, blazer, denim, overcoat and dress shirts. The data backs blazer (15.4% → 17.1% of looks) and denim (11.6% → 13.1%). Leather (12.1%) and overcoat (17.0%) held steady.
